How this works

A fully automated screen of ~6,000 US companies runs every weekday, scoring each one across 12 classic value and growth frameworks (Graham, Buffett, Lynch, and others) plus regulatory-filing signals. Each month, the paper portfolio buys the top 20 names, equal weight, and rebalances on the last trading day. No human stock-picking, no overrides — every order the system has ever placed is in the public ledger.

Before any paper trading began, the engine was backtested over 2013–2025 under pre-registered, locked pass/fail criteria — the rules were written down and committed before results existed, and the losing variants are published alongside the winner. Those simulated historical results live in the repository (criteria, verdict report, failure analysis) and are not mixed into the live curve above — everything on this page is paper trading from 2026-07-22 onward.

Not investment advice. This is an educational and informational project. Simulated (paper) trading results do not represent real trading and no real capital is at risk; nothing here is a recommendation to buy or sell any security. Do your own research.
